Frequently Asked Questions

Which booking option should I choose?

Use the Quick Interest Form if you're in the early stages of planning and aren't sure about exact dates, locations, or packages. Choose the Detailed Booking Form if you already know exactly what you want and are ready to secure a specific date and time.

How far in advance should I book?

For graduation photos during peak season (April-May), I recommend booking 3-4 weeks in advance. For other sessions, 1-2 weeks is usually sufficient. Last-minute bookings are sometimes possible - just use the Quick Interest Form to check availability.

Do I need to pay when booking?

No payment is required during the initial booking process. After form submission and confirmation of details, a 50% deposit is required to secure your date. The remaining balance is due on the day of your session.
